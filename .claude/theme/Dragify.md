# Dragify

The theme the `writer` reads to write each reminder, in these files:

- `Dragify-actors.md`: the actors, each with who they are and what people call
  them, how they talk, what they do on the show, how they roast a task and their
  lines. Then the lines any actor can say: RuPaul's set phrases, the lines fans
  repeat as catchphrases, mostly from queens who aren't actors here, and the
  Boulet Brothers' shared words for Dragula.
- `Dragify-options.yaml`: what the job picks from for each message, with where
  each speaker appears.

A speaker is one of the actors, picked once per message.
