# Agents

Behind the skills, three agents each do one job. A skill or scheduled job asks
an agent when it needs that job done; you don't run one yourself. The writer
sends you your reminders. The estimator and the shopper hand their answers back
to the skill that asked.

## The writer

The writer writes and sends your reminders and your weekly plan to your phone.
It has one question in mind: what will make you actually read this one? It
writes each message in the style you set up, takes its tone from the day, and
sends it the way you chose to be notified. It has no fixed shape, because the
same message every day stops getting read.

## The estimator

The estimator works out how big each task is, on your own scale, and whether you
could do all of it in one go after a workday. When you review your task list
with ntm, it proposes each task's estimate and you decide. It learns from the
estimates you decide on, so its guesses get closer to yours over time.

Planning uses the estimates to pick tasks that fit your day.

## The shopper

The shopper checks one thing you're watching to buy: is it a good deal today? It
looks across stores you'd trust, checks today's price on each store's own page,
and notes what it found on the task.

The daily price watch asks a shopper about each item you're watching, then sends
you one push with each deal's price, store and a link to the store's page.
