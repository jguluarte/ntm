## What's new

`ntm:self-test`, `ntm:style` and the two skills in your own plugin,
`ntm-user:notify` and `ntm-user:backend`, are out of the `/` menu. To run the
self-test or get something written in your style, ask in chat, such as "run the
ntm self-test".

Each skill in the `/` menu now describes itself the way `/ntm:help` does.
