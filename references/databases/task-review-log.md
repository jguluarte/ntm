# Task review log

_How estimating works with you, learned from each review. The estimator agent
creates this database from ntm's template, reads it whenever it estimates, and
keeps it as you review; change it to change how it estimates for you. Delete a
part to go back to ntm's default._

## How you work

_What the estimator has learned about how you size and break up tasks, so review
can come to propose most of your list the way you would. Short bullets, written
to you, under the topic they belong to. Learned from what you change, push back
on or say. Kept tidy, not short: related bullets fold together, and a change
rewrites a bullet rather than adding one._

## Estimates

_One line per settled estimate, newest first: the date it was settled, the
task's id, the estimator's last guess, what you settled on, and a few words on
what made you change it or what made the guess right. Guesses and finals are
written on your scale. The newest 150 are kept. A line looks like
`{"date":"2026-11-09","id":"ABC-12","guess":"2 hours","final":"3 hours","why":"wiring was slow"}`._
