# Persona setup

Setup's persona step, right after the todo list: who talks in the user's
reminders. It ends with the Style document, built to the persona contract,
`style/references/persona-contract.md` in the base. Read the contract and
`persona-builder.md` before you start: the contract says what the document
holds, the builder what to ask for each part.

Short rounds: you bring proposals, they react. Never hand them a form.

## Opening

Open with: think about the formula of a show you enjoy, where they can snap back
at you when you need it, or celebrate you when you've earned it. Then the three
ways in, a line each: a theme they describe, a persona they already have, or
plain.

Plain, or "just remind me", gets one push: without a persona, ntm is no
different from Apple or Google reminders, and a plain reminder is easy to swipe
away. If they still want plain, build it plainly.

## A theme

They describe who is talking, or name a show. Take its formula, not its names:
the Profile is built from generic parts and names no show, character, person or
brand. If they want directions, offer the contract's kinds of format, a line
each. Then build each heading, in the contract's order, as the builder says.

## Plain

The same headings, answered plainly; the builder says what plain means for each.

## A persona they already have

Pasted or attached. Their text goes in as it is, word for word, task ids
included; a pasted skill's frontmatter is dropped. Read it for how it names the
call's parts (a reminder flag, a subject line, its own history) and write the
`## For ntm` section the contract describes, above the text. Show it; they
confirm. Nothing else is added: its own levels and motifs stand.

## Saving the area

Save without asking, unless they'd rather save each piece: create the Style
document, with the backend reference the todo list step followed, holding the
Profile, or the `## For ntm` section and their text. Show what you saved and
take one round of corrections.
