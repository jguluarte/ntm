## What's new

This version is all about your theme.

Your reminders stay fresh. Each message is built from a theme with many pieces
to draw on: who's talking, the scenes, the running bits. A new mix of them
starts every message, so the same line doesn't keep coming back.

The theme builder sets your theme up with you, one part at a time, from a world
you enjoy. You can keep several themes, and ntm takes them in turn, a week each.

Your messages learn from what worked: the pieces that got you moving come up
more often.

Also: a deal alert from the price watch now links straight to the store.

## For ntm:update

A 0.4 setup differs from a 0.5 one here:

| In 0.4                                     | In 0.5                                      | Step |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| Database names in `ntm-user:backend`       | Under Databases in `%Settings`              | 1    |
| One `%Theme` database, in parts            | A theme's files, listed under Themes        | 2    |
| One `%Message-history`                     | One per theme, made by the theme builder    | 2    |
| Reminder rules that read the history       | Slips read from each task's own dates       | 3    |
| A weekly setup job that asks the messenger | Rotates themes, refreshes picks, then sends | 4    |

Their reminder and price watch jobs keep the same steps. Tell the user in a line
what each step does as it starts.

### 1. Name the databases in `%Settings`

Add the Databases and Themes parts to `%Settings`, as setup's template
(`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/setup/references/databases/settings.md`) has
them. Under Databases, write each database the template lists with its real
name, from where `ntm-user:backend` says it lives. Leave Themes empty. Add
Databases only when `%Settings` has Planning. It ends when `%Settings` is saved.

### 2. Rebuild their theme

Read their 0.4 theme, the database `ntm-user:backend` lists as `%Theme`. Load
`ntm:theme-builder` and follow it through its Result, with that theme as the
theme they already have: its guide sorts it into the new files and builds what's
missing with them. Write the theme's root over their 0.4 `%Theme`, and its
message history over their 0.4 `%Message-history`, each retitled as the theme
builder names it. It ends when the theme builder switches the theme on.

### 3. Bring the reminder rules up to date

When they have `%Message-rules`, write its Reminders and Morning parts as
setup's template has them, keeping anything they added to them. The template is
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/setup/references/databases/message-rules.md`. It
ends when `%Message-rules` is saved.

### 4. Fix the weekly setup job

When they have Planning in `%Settings`, find the weekly setup job among their
scheduled tasks, by the `ntm:` skills it runs. Write its prompt as setup's
Create the jobs builds it from the Weekly setup steps, keeping its title, time,
timezone, model and any step they added. It ends when the job is saved.

When you can't edit a scheduled task from here, give them the prompt to paste
into the job themselves.
