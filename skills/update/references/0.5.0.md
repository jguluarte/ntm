## What's new

This version is all about your theme.

Your reminders stay fresh. Each message is built from a theme with many pieces
to draw on: who's talking, the scenes, the running bits. A new mix of them
starts every message, so the same line doesn't keep coming back.

The theme builder sets your theme up with you, one part at a time, from a world
you enjoy. You can keep several themes, and ntm takes them in turn, a week each.

Your messages learn from what worked: the pieces that got you moving come up
more often.

Also: a deal alert from the price watch now links straight to the store.

## For ntm:update

A 0.4 setup differs from a 0.5 one here:

| In 0.4                                     | In 0.5                                      | Step |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ---- |
| Database names in `ntm-user:backend`       | Under Databases in `%Settings`              | 1    |
| One `%Theme` database, in parts            | A theme's files, listed under Themes        | 2    |
| One `%Message-history`                     | One per theme, made by the theme builder    | 2, 5 |
| Reminder rules that read the history       | Slips read from each task's own dates       | 3    |
| A weekly setup job that asks the messenger | Rotates themes, refreshes picks, then sends | 4    |

Their reminder and price watch jobs keep the same steps. An offer that gets a no
ends its step, with nothing changed.

### 1. Name the databases in `%Settings`

Add the Databases and Themes parts to `%Settings`, as setup's template
(`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/setup/references/databases/settings.md`) has
them. Under Databases, write each database the template lists with its real
name, from where `ntm-user:backend` says it lives. Leave Themes empty. Add
Databases only when `%Settings` has Planning. It ends when `%Settings` is saved.

### 2. Build their theme

An offer: rebuild their theme in the new shape with the theme builder, starting
from the theme they have.

On their yes, read their 0.4 theme: the database `ntm-user:backend` lists as
`%Theme`. Load `ntm:theme-builder` and follow it through its Result, with that
theme as the theme they already have: its guide sorts it into the new files and
builds what's missing with them. It ends when the theme builder switches the
theme on.

On a no, tell them their messages need a theme in the new shape, and that
`/ntm:theme-builder` builds it whenever they're ready.

### 3. Bring the reminder rules up to date

An offer, when they have `%Message-rules`. Their Reminders and Morning rules
read the old message history; the new ones read each task's slips from the task
itself. Show them the template's Reminders and Morning parts
(`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/setup/references/databases/message-rules.md`). On
their yes, write those two parts, keeping anything they added to them.

### 4. Update the weekly setup job

An offer, when they have Planning in `%Settings`. Find the weekly setup job
among their scheduled tasks, by the `ntm:` skills it runs. Its prompt becomes
the one setup's Create the jobs builds from the Weekly setup steps, with its
title, time, timezone and model kept. Keep any step they added. Show them the
new prompt, and on their yes, write it into the job.

When you can't edit a scheduled task from here, give them the prompt to paste
into the job themselves.

### 5. Clear out the old theme

An offer, only when step 2 switched a theme on. Their 0.4 `%Theme` and
`%Message-history` databases are no longer read. On their yes, delete both.

Rebuild ntm-user: yes
