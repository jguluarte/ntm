## What's new

This version is all about your theme.

Your reminders stay fresh: each message draws a new mix from your theme, such as
who's talking, the scenes and the running bits, so the same line doesn't keep
coming back.

The theme builder sets up a theme with you from a world you enjoy. Keep several,
and ntm takes them in turn, a week each.

Your messages learn from what worked: the pieces that got you moving come up
more often.

Also new: a deal alert from the price watch links straight to the store.

## For ntm:update

Tell the user in a line what each step does as it starts. Setup's database
templates are in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/setup/references/databases/`.

### 1. Name the databases in `%Settings`

Add the Themes part to `%Settings` as setup's `settings.md` template has it.
When `%Settings` has Planning, add the Databases part too, with each database's
real name from where `ntm-user:backend` says it lives.

### 2. Rebuild their theme

Load `ntm:theme-builder` and follow it, skipping its introduction, to build from
their 0.4 theme, the database `ntm-user:backend` lists as `%Theme`. Ask the user
only what that theme leaves open. Once the new theme is switched on, delete the
0.4 `%Theme` and `%Message-history`.

### 3. Bring the reminder rules up to date

When they have `%Message-rules`, write its Reminders and Morning parts as
setup's `message-rules.md` template has them, keeping anything they added to
them.

### 4. Fix the weekly setup job

When they have Planning in `%Settings`, find the weekly setup job among their
scheduled tasks, by the `ntm:` skills it runs. Write its prompt as setup's
Create the jobs builds it from the Weekly setup steps, keeping its title, time,
timezone, model and any step they added.

When you can't edit a scheduled task from here, give them the prompt to paste
into the job themselves.
