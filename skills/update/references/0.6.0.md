## What's new

On Linear, closed tasks move out of their projects each week, each with a
comment that links back to its project. On the free plan, this saves your task
limit for the work still open.

The weekly learning reaches your reminders: the parts of your theme that got you
moving come up more often.

The skills your jobs run, such as your reminders, are out of the `/` menu. To
run one yourself, ask for it in chat.

When ntm brings your setup up to a new version on its own, the job that did it
says so.
